# RAT GOD — SIM ⇄ RENDER contract v2

Owner: the **SIM stream**. The render and audio streams build against it.
Version: **2.2.0** (see the changelog at the bottom). The machine-readable source of truth is **`src/sim/v2/contract.ts`** (types only). Shared pure helpers are in **`src/sim/v2/shared.ts`**: materials, spline sampling, strides and speeds.
The worker entry is **`src/sim/v2/worker.ts`**, with messages `ToWorker2` and `FromWorker2`. The v1 sim (`src/sim/*.ts`, `src/net/protocol.ts`) stays untouched and keeps working until render switches `main.ts` to v2.

```ts
const w = new Worker(new URL('./sim/v2/worker.ts', import.meta.url), { type: 'module' });
w.postMessage({ t: 'new', opts: { seed: 1234 } });   // → { t:'init', terrain, burrow, powers, laws } then a stream of { t:'snap' }
```

## 1. Units, time, cadence
| Thing | Value |
|---|---|
| Tile | **4 cm**. Terrain is a W×H grid (default **320×180** = 12.8 m × 7.2 m). y grows down. `surfaceY[x]` is the ground line. |
| Rat | An adult body (no tail) is **~5 tiles long and ~1.5 tiles tall**, scaled by `size` (0.35 newborn … 1.15 big adult). The tail adds about 4 tiles. |
| Tunnel | **2–3 tiles** clear width (one lane for an adult; opposite traffic must *squeeze*). Galleries are 3–4 tiles. Chambers are 4–10 tiles tall and 10–40 wide. |
| Tick | **0.25 s of wall time at 1×**. Speeds are 0, 0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 4 and 8. |
| Day | **960 ticks**: 4 min at 1× or 30 s at 8×. 40 ticks per hour. A season is 10 days and a year is 40 days. |
| Snapshots | One per tick, capped at **~15 Hz** of wall time. At 4× and 8× the worker coalesces ticks. `snap.tickMs` is the wall ms per tick at the current speed. |

**Render interpolation rule:** keep the last two snapshots and interpolate `x`, `y` and `heading` by wall time (`(now - tRecv) / (snapshot interval)`). For very fast motion, sample the edge spline at the interpolated `s` instead of lerping x/y (`sampleEdge(edges[edge], s)`) whenever `edge` is unchanged between the two snapshots. That way bodies follow the curve exactly and never cut through earth.

**Gait rule (fixes the "superfast jitter loops"):** walking, running, climbing and squeezing cycles are driven by **distance, not time**: `phase = (dist / STRIDE_TILES[stage]) % 1`. A queued or stopped rat (`speed≈0`) holds its pose and plays its idle (breathing, sniffing) loop at a calm, fixed rate. Idle-type loops (eat, groom, sleep, dig) should run at **4–8 fps** of sprite time, independent of the sim speed up to 2×. At higher speeds they may scale with `min(speed, 3)`.

## 2. Terrain (`init.terrain`, `snap.terrain` deltas)
Per tile, all as Uint8 0..255 unless noted:
- `mat`: one of `Mat.Air`, `Topsoil`, `Loam`, `Clay`, `Gravel`, `Roots`, `Rock`, `Pipe`, `Concrete`, `Bedrock`. Hardness, permeability and colour hints are in `MATERIALS`. Rock, pipe, concrete and bedrock are not diggable, so tunnels route around them.
- `carve`: 0 = solid; 1–199 = a **partially gnawed face** (show cracks, crumbling and a darker lip); **≥ 200 (`PASSABLE`) = open void**. Carving is gradual: a digger raises `carve` on the frontier tiles a little every tick.
- `debris`: loose spoil on the floor. It piles up behind diggers until haulers carry it out. Render it as dirt mounds and pebbles.
- `water`: standing water in the tile, from flooding (255 = full). Render a surface line and flow; rats swim (`Anim.Swim`).
- `moist`: soil wetness, from rain soaking through permeable soil. Render a darker, glistening tint. It raises collapse risk and disease.
- `stab`: structural stability. **Below 60, show cracks and trickling dust; below 25 the tile is about to collapse.** A collapse refills `carve` toward 0 and dumps `debris` (event `collapse`).
- Static features: `pipes` (segments with radius; `leaking` pipes drip water), `roots` (polylines) and `rocks` (ellipses). They already match `mat`, so render can draw them nicely.

`TerrainDelta` holds the tiles changed since the last snapshot as parallel arrays `idx`, `carve`, `water`, `debris`, `stab` and `moist`.

## 3. Burrow graph (`init.burrow`, `snap.burrow` deltas)
The burrow is **procedural** and follows real *Rattus norvegicus* burrow structure:
- entry holes at the surface, often under cover (pipe ends, kerbs);
- a sloping entry tunnel;
- branching galleries;
- **bolt-holes**: blind tunnels that end just under the surface and are opened in emergencies;
- **nest chambers** lined with bedding;
- **food caches**;
- **latrines** (side pockets);
- **dead ends**.

The city then grows civic chambers:
- throne
- granary
- nursery
- plaza
- shrine
- barracks
- midden
- infirmary
- market
- workshop
- slum

Tunnels are generated by a soil-aware planner. They avoid rock and pipes, prefer soft loam, follow root lines, wobble organically, and slope at most about 40° except for shafts.

- **`NavNode`**: a junction, chamber entry, entrance or surface point, with `{id, x, y, kind}`.
- **`NavEdge`**: `{id, a, b, kind: tunnel|floor|shaft|surface, pts, len, width, chamber, dug, from}`.
  - `pts` are control points; the spline is **Catmull-Rom, arc-length parametrised** (`shared.ts → sampleEdge(edge, s)`).
  - `floor` edges are the walkable floor line of a chamber.
  - **`dug`** is how many tiles from node `from` have been excavated. Rats can only stand on `s` within the dug part. While `dug < len` the edge is a **dig front**: render the gnawed face at `s = dug` (or `len - dug` if `from == b`).
- **`Chamber`**: `{id, kind, name, cx, cy, rx, ry, seed, floorY, floorEdge, entryNode, progress, stock, owner, flooded}`. The shape is defined by the carve field, so the ellipse is only a hint for labels and lighting. `stock` is food for granary and caches, and spoil or corpses for the midden.
- **Deltas** (`BurrowDelta`): new nodes, edges and chambers as they are planned; `edgeDug` progress; and chamber `[id, progress, stock, owner, flooded]`.

**Invariant (tested):** every rat with `edge >= 0` is at a position whose tiles have `carve >= PASSABLE`. Rats never teleport, so consecutive positions are continuous along the graph, and they never cross solid ground. The only off-graph positions (`edge = -1`) are on the surface street (y < surfaceY), where rats walk on the ground line.

## 4. Rats (`snap.rats`, a structure of arrays)
Fields: `id`, `x`, `y` (feet, tiles), `heading` (rad), `facing` (±1), `speed` (tiles/tick), `dist` (gait clock), `edge`, `s`, `lane` (lateral offset), `anim` (`Anim`), `stage` (`Stage`), `carry` (`Carry`), `size`, `coat` (0–7, heritable), `faction`, `dynasty`, `flags` (`RF` bits), `mood` (−100..100), `target` (rat id they interact with).

**Movement model** (what render will see):
- **Acceleration and braking**: about 0.25 tiles/tick².
- **Speeds**: in `SPEEDS`, slowed on slopes, in water and when carrying.
- **Queueing**: followers keep about 1.1 body lengths behind; `RF.queued` is set when blocked.
- **Opposite-direction passing**: in one-lane tunnels the two rats both slow to squeeze speed, take `lane = ±0.5` and play `Anim.Squeeze`. In wide edges they pass on lanes without slowing.
- **Chamber floors**: rats spread over the floor and huddle to sleep (piles).
- **Climbing shafts**: `Anim.Climb`, with heading about ±π/2.

**Animation intent:** `anim` is the action; `carry` adds a held item (food, scrap, debris, pup in mouth, corpse dragged, poster); `stage` picks pup, juvenile, adult or elder art; `flags` add overlays (sick → fleas, wet → drips, king → circlet, blessed → faint halo, cursed → dark motes, risen → pale eyes). `Anim.Dig` happens at a dig front, facing the face, and render should puff debris. `Anim.Haul` combined with `Carry.Debris` is a rat pushing spoil backward.

**LOD:** render should send `{t:'view', x0, y0, x1, y1}` (tiles) when the camera moves. Rats outside the view (plus a margin) carry `RF.lod`: their bodies update every 4 ticks with no crowd interaction, but their minds, lives and identities are fully simulated. Positions stay continuous.

## 5. Entities, fields, environment
- **`hazards`**: cat, snake, exterminator, drone, bait, boot, owl and ferret, each with `x`, `y`, `edge`, `s`, `facing`, `anim` and `target`. Snakes and ferrets **enter tunnels** and move along edges like rats. Cats wait at entrances and paw in. Exterminators gas entrances.
- **`corpses`**: `{x, y, edge, s, age, cause, violent, decay, stage, coat, size}`. Respect the reduce-gore toggle on the render side.
- **`items`**: food, scrap, bait, poster and nest bedding on the ground.
- **`posters`**: law posters pasted by heralds, with `chamber`, position and `torn`.
- **`fields`**: a coarse (W/4 × H/4) **gas**, **smoke**, **fire**, **scent** and **light** grid, sent only when non-empty. Use it for volumetric effects.
- **`env`**: `tod` (0 midnight, .25 dawn, .5 noon, .75 dusk), `hour`, `season`, `weather` (clear, overcast, fog, rain, storm, heat, cold, snow, drought), `intensity`, `rain`, `wind`, `tempC`, `sunlight`, `lightning` (strike this snapshot), `streetActivity` (traffic of the Giants) and `quake` (camera shake 0..1). Weather affects the sim (water inflow, moisture, foraging risk, sleep, disease), so the render only needs to visualise it.

## 6. Society, king, feeds
- **`stats`** (`SocietyStats`): population, food, granary, scrap, market `foodPrice`, `gini`, `legitimacy`, `trust`, `unrest`, `faith`, `cohesion`, `crime`, `disease`, `hunger`, the class breakdown, and the god's `grace` / `graceMax`.
- **`king`** (`KingInfo`): the same concept as v1 (persona, thought, plate, reaching/pressing, active laws with `adopt` share). The king is autonomous and never leaves the throne chamber. Laws spread by **heralds** (rats with `RF.herald` and `Carry.Poster`, playing `Anim.Paste` at a wall) with lag.
- **`factions`**: emergent groups — royalists, zealots, dissidents, reformers, guild, gang, cult and clan — with `leader`, `members`, `color` (tint for badges), `creed`, `aims`, `stance` and `parent` (for schisms).
- **`prayers`**: open prayers from rats to the god (`plea`, `kind`, position). Show them as a feed and as little glyphs above the praying rat. They are the player's main prompt to act.
- **`watch`**: the **"watch this" suggestion feed**, `{ratId, arc, reason, score}`.
- **`arcs`**: detected story arcs. Kinds:

  | Kind | Kind | Kind |
  |---|---|---|
  | rise_to_power | forbidden_love | infidelity |
  | martyr | famine | schism |
  | feud | coup | assassination |
  | plague | prophet | revenge |
  | dynasty_fall | exodus | crime_wave |
  | reform | tyrant | saviour |
  | disaster | miracle | orphan |
  | rivalry | | |

  Each has a `title`, `rats`, `stage`, a `heat` (0..1, for sorting) and `ended`.
- **`gathering`**: a vote, riot, coup, funeral or sermon, with `chamber`, `votes` [kill, spare], `of` and `endsAt`.
- **`events`** (`SimEvent2`): new events since the last snapshot. Severity drives the UI: 0 = ticker, 1 = chronicle, 2 = toast or notification, 3 = epochal modal or card. Each carries optional `rats`, `x`, `y`, `chamber`, `arc`, `fx` (a render effect hint, e.g. `lightning`, `dust_burst`, `splash`, `halo`, `blood`, `smoke`) and `sfx` (an audio hint, e.g. `thunder`, `collapse`, `squeal`, `choir`, `bell`). A `card` id maps to the event-card art.

## 7. Inspect, life stories, chronicle
- `{t:'inspect', id}` attaches a **`RatDetail`** to every snapshot until `id: null`. It includes the name, title, lineage, role, class, faction, Big-Five personality, genes, needs, skills, beliefs (crown, god, equality, order, kin), blessings and curses, relations (strongest 12, with kinds such as kin, mate, pairbond, friend, rival, mentor, mentee, debtor, creditor, grudge and lover), memories, and **`story`**: the life story as paragraphs generated from what actually happened.
- `{t:'story', id}` returns `{t:'story', detail}` once, for any rat, alive or dead. Every life is kept.
- `{t:'chronicle'}` returns **`ChronicleV2`**: reigns, dynasties, arcs, god acts, deaths by cause, and the day-by-day history of pop, food, gini, legitimacy, faith and unrest.

## 8. God powers
Command: `{ t:'god', req, power, target: GodTarget, params?: GodParams }`. The worker answers with **`GodResult`** `{req, ok, reason?, cost, grace, effects[], witnesses, events[], forecast?}` and emits world events (always at least one `god_act` event, with an `fx` hint).

**Grace** is the god's power pool (`stats.grace` / `graceMax`, 100). It regenerates slowly on its own and faster the more rats believe, because worship feeds the god. A new game with `opts.unbound: true` ignores grace, so the player is truly not bound by any rule. Without grace a power still fails cleanly (`ok:false, reason:'grace'`).

**Faith in god (the core ripple):**
- Every act is perceived by witnesses: rats within sight and hearing (the same chamber or tunnel, about 30 tiles) plus anyone who later hears the story through rumours.
- Each witness interprets the act through its personality, current beliefs and memories. A blessing seen by a skeptic can read as a trick; a smiting seen by a zealot reads as justice.
- Interpretations update the rat's `beliefs.god` and an `image` of the god (benevolent, wrathful, capricious).
- Zealots, cults, heretics, priests and prophets emerge from this.
- `stats.faith` is the mean. Rats **pray** when desperate and faithful, and the player sees `prayers`. An answered prayer — a god act that fixes the plea — produces strong conversion.


## 8b. Knowledge, institutions, culture (2.1)

**Lore rule for all game text:** rats do not know the word for the makers.
- The makers are **the Giants** (or **the Gloves**).
- The lab is **the White Place**.
- Never use human, people, man, woman, person, or similar words in any string the sim emits. A test greps the sim's text tables for these words.

**Knowledge (`snap.tech`, `snap.research`)**
- An open-ended graph of about 40 techs across eras 0–5. Prerequisites are *any-of* groups, so many routes reach the same tech.
- Examples, with what each needs:
  - era 0: fire, tools, preservation (needs salt or cold), marks (counting, then writing);
  - era 1: medicine, pottery, wheels, lanterns;
  - era 2: scrap-smithing, traps, weapons, sanitation;
  - era 3: sparks (electricity tapped from street cables);
  - era 4: clockwork, radio;
  - era 5: **RI (ratificial intelligence)** and **rodentoids**.
- Discovery is a per-rat stochastic event that weighs:
  - curiosity (openness) and intellect gene;
  - need pressure (famine → preservation, plague → medicine, war → weapons);
  - accidents (fire from a sparking cable in a storm), and observation of hazards, the Giants and other rats;
  - failed experiments, which leave partial progress that a mentee can continue;
  - the tools and materials at hand.
- Knowledge lives in knowers. It spreads by teaching (mentorship, lectures, written marks if `marks` is known) and is **lost** when the last knower dies.
- Factions, firms or religions may keep it **secret**, **ban** it (the king or a party), **steal** it, or **worship** it.
- **Lab rats** (`RF.labrat`) are an *emergent, not guaranteed* arrival of pale, tagged rats from the White Place. Their chance rises with street activity and seasons. They bring partial knowledge of advanced techs, but their trust in the colony is low.

**Rodentoids** (`RF.rodentoid`, in the normal `RatsBlock`, `stage = Adult`, `coat` = model 0..3)
- They are built by a firm that knows `rodentoids` and has scrap and sparks. They take menial jobs: dig, haul, wheel, forage.
- They break down and need repair (`Anim.Repair`, `Anim.Charge`).
- Workers displaced by them react from their own state. Some welcome the leisure; insecure rats (high N, low wealth) may form unions, strike, protest or sabotage. Nothing is scripted.

**Firms (`snap.firms`)**
- Jobs are firms: diggers, foragers, scrapworks, granary, wheelworks, healers, builders, smithy, sparkworks, rodentoid works, scribes, brewers, traders and guards.
- Each firm has a boss, workers, a wage in food per day, wealth, a market share (≥ 0.7 means monopoly) and an optional union.
- Firms are founded by enterprising rats. They hire, fire, cut wages, compete, merge and go bust.

**Parties and council (`snap.parties`, `snap.council`)**
- Parties form from shared beliefs and platforms, for example "OPEN THE GRANARY", "BAN THE MACHINES" or "THE GOD ABOVE THE CROWN".
- A council with elections can emerge, through a reform movement or the *free election* law. The king still never leaves the throne.
- Lobbying: a firm funds a party as its `patron`. Corruption is tracked per party (`corrupt`). Parties can be banned by crackdowns.

**Justice (`snap.justice`)**
- Enforcers arrest suspects on evidence or rumour, so wrongful convictions happen.
- A trial runs as a gathering of kind `trial`, judged by the king's appointee or the council. The verdict is jail with a sentence, exile, execution, or release.
- Jail is a chamber; inmates may form prison gangs or escape. Pardons can come from the king, the council or a god act.
- Secret police may emerge under paranoid kings.

**Religions (`snap.religions`)**
- Religions *may* form from god acts, prophets, lab-rat "miracles" (technology seen as magic), floods or ancestor worship.
- Each has a deity, doctrines, temples, a tolerance value (−1 persecuting … 1 harmonious), schisms (`parent`) and holy wars (`enemies`).
- Faiths can kill each other, or live in harmony.

**Festivals and everyday life (`snap.festivals`)**
- Festivals emerge from plenty, crownings, a saint's day after a martyr, midwinter, mourning after a flood, games, weddings, victories and machine day.
- Everyday life includes pups playing (`Anim.Play`), courtship (`Anim.Kiss`), gossip (`Anim.Talk`), music, dance and games, art and graffiti (`Anim.Graffiti`; a graffiti `Item` is left on the wall), and writing marks.

**Decision model (every agent, including the king)**
- Each choice is a softmax over options.
- Option utility = *intuition* + *facts*:
  - **intuition**: personality, current emotions, biases such as loss aversion and in-group favour, salient memories, and rumours heard;
  - **facts**: the rat's **own perceived** state, i.e. local food, what it has seen, knowledge, and remembered outcomes of its past choices.
- Information is imperfect and local, so two rats in the same place can choose differently, and every choice depends on earlier ones.
- `RatDetail.lastDecision` exposes the latest mix (`intuition` and `facts` weights and a `why` string), so the inspect sheet can show *why*.

**Render additions in 2.1:**
- rodentoid sprites (4 models) and lab-rat sprites;
- tool props (`RatsBlock.tool`);
- the new anims (Experiment, Write, Graffiti, Dance, Music, Game, Jailed, Strike, Trial, Lecture, Repair, Charge, Tend, Kiss);
- jail bars on the jail chamber;
- temple decor for chambers listed in `religions[].temples`;
- the festival look (lights and bunting) for `festivals[].chamber`;
- UI panels for Knowledge, Economy (firms), Politics (parties and council), Faiths and Justice;
- new event kinds and arc kinds to surface.

## 8c. God against king, family life, castes, mature systems, ripples, tiers (2.2)

**Punish (`GodPower 'punish'`, cost 20)**
- Target: any rat, most often the king.
- Parameter `punishment`: `pain`, `humble`, `blight` (his stores rot), `strip` (his court flees), or `nightmare`.
- The outcome is emergent, from the king's persona, faith and current state, and from the colony's faith distribution (`stats.faithDist`):
  - **repentance**: he repeals cruel laws and performs mercy acts;
  - **fear** or **indifference**;
  - **defiance**: he bans the faiths and burns the shrines;
  - **war on the god** (`KingInfo.stanceToGod = 'at_war'`): decrees against prayer, executions of priests, "fortifying" against the sky.
- **If the colony is devout**, the faithful may turn on a defiant king. This raises unrest and can lead to a coup or regicide (`regicide` event, `god_vs_king` arc).
- **If the colony is mostly doubters**, it may reject the god (`apostasy`, `stats.godRejection`). An **atheist** faction (`FactionKind 'atheists'`, aim *PROVE THE WORLD IS REAL*) then does defiant things. The `prove_reality` events include:
  - deliberately breaking taboos;
  - daring the sky on the surface at noon;
  - smashing idols;
  - "experiments" to test whether prayers do anything.
- Grace regenerates more slowly as rejection grows.

**Ripples (`snap.ripples`, `SimEvent2.ripples`, `RatDetail.ripples`, `ChronicleV2.ripples`)**
- Every god act opens a Ripple. Rats touched by the act carry its id.
- Events involving tagged rats (and events caused by state the act changed) inherit the ids. Depth increases at each hop, and the tag decays after about 4 hops or about 6 days.
- The UI can show "this happened because you did X" by reading `Ripple.chain`.

**Importance tiers (`SimEvent2.imp`, `SimEvent2.score`)**
- Every event has a 0..100 score: base by kind, plus severity, plus rats watched or royal, plus ripple involvement.
- Tiers: `minor` < 35 ≤ `notable` < 70 ≤ `major`.
- Suggested UI handling: minor events go to the ambient feed, notable ones to the pill, major ones to a cinematic banner and the Omens inbox. Text is never truncated.

**Housing era (`snap.homes`, `ChamberKind 'home'`, `RatsBlock.home`)**
- **When it starts:** once the colony can sustain it (pop ≥ about 40, granary surplus, digging capacity, at least one pair bond).
- **Building:** bonded pairs marry (`marriage` event; ceremony gathering in the plaza or temple) and plan their own small chamber. The pair dig it themselves, with friends and kin helping.
- **`HomeInfo` contents:**
  - owners and family;
  - wealth (relative to the city);
  - caste and faith;
  - culture tags, from skills, beliefs and jobs;
  - style axes (tidy, bold, warm, odd, grand), from personality and wealth;
  - history lines;
  - possessions (kind, quality, origin), gathered over a lifetime as gifts, loot, tech and inheritance.
- **Render:** compose a unique interior from the prop kit using `seed`, `style`, `culture` and `possessions`.
- **Family dynamics:**
  - parenting quality, which affects pups' beliefs and skills;
  - sibling rivalry;
  - inheritance (home and possessions pass to a child or spouse, and disputes are possible);
  - divorce and widowhood;
  - remarriage.

**Castes (`snap.castes`, `RatsBlock.caste`)**
- **Emergence:** a caste system may emerge only with homes plus inheritance, when there are strong `order` and `kin` beliefs and visible occupational inheritance.
- **Assignment:** a rat's caste follows its parents' caste and occupation.
- **Norms:** each caste has purity norms such as "MARRY WITHIN" or "THE DEAD-HANDLERS ARE UNCLEAN".
- **Mobility:** rats can rise through wealth, marriage or merit, and can fall through crime or poverty.
- **Discrimination:** hiring, trials, rations and courtship are biased by caste.
- **Conflict:** caste conflict and caste wars can follow.

**Mature systems (content-gated, never required for play)**
- **Romance and jealousy:**
  - romance, courtship and mating;
  - affairs (the existing systems);
  - sexual jealousy, which can lead to fights or murder.
- **Incest taboo, with real inbreeding genetics:**
  - each rat has an inbreeding coefficient (`RatDetail.inbreeding`);
  - inbred pups get recessive disorders: lower health, fertility and longevity;
  - kin matings that are discovered cause scandal and shunning.
- **Scandal and shunning:** shunned rats are ignored, refused trade and refused courtship.
- **Depiction:**
  - mating is *implied*: `Anim.Mate`, events tagged `mature:['sex']`; the render uses a fade, nest shaking or hearts;
  - violence is tagged `violence` / `gore`, and incest `incest`.
- **War carnage:**
  - `RatsBlock.wounds` holds 2 bits per body part (`BODY_PARTS`), plus `RatsBlock.blood`;
  - `Corpse.wounds` and `Corpse.gore`;
  - severed parts are `Item{kind:'limb', variant: part, mature:true}`, and blood pools are `Item{kind:'blood'}`.
- **Consent:** the UI sends `{t:'consent', consent:{mature, gore 0|1|2, sexual}}`; the default is all off.
  - The sim writes event `text` in the matching wording; safe wording is used when a tag is not consented.
  - The render must hide tagged fx, items and corpse detail itself.

**Prayers as nudges:** `Prayer.suggest` names the power a prayer implicitly asks for. For example, a rat praying for rain suggests `storm`. The UI can use this for diegetic prompts, with no tutorial.

## 8d. World Bible merge (2.2.0 final, V2_SCOPE_DELTA §1.9)
`docs/WORLD_BIBLE.md` is the authoritative design; this section adopts the delta's proposed contract fields. **All additions are optional and additive**; nothing from the 2.2.0 draft was removed or renamed.

**Events (`SimEvent2`)** — every event now also carries:
- `tier` ('minor' | 'notable' | 'major'), identical to `imp`. Drive the tiered UI (bible L14.6) from either.
- `title` (≤ 48 chars) and `body` (≤ 220 chars). Never cut them with an ellipsis; wrap instead.
- `portraitRat?`, `artKey?` (event-art id for MAJOR cards), `stinger?` (audio stinger id).
- `cause?` — the causal id of this event: `G#n` (god act n), `E#seq` (another event), or `N` (nature).
- `godRoot?` — the god act it descends from (`G#n`), if any.
- `content?: {sexual?, gore?: 0|1|2}` — the delta's object form of the `mature` tags (both are sent).
- `textFull?` — when consent replaced the wording, the unfiltered text. The simulation itself never changes with content settings; only wording does.

**New worker messages**
- `{t:'ripple', card: RippleCard}` is pushed when a sev ≥ 2 event has a god root at least 1 day old, at most once per in-game hour (bible L12.8). `RippleCard {id, rootAct:'G#n', power, tick, text, chain[]}`.
- `{t:'omens'}` → `{t:'omens', list, unread}`: the unread MAJOR events. `{t:'omensRead', seqs}` marks them read. `snap.omensUnread` carries the badge count.
- `{t:'ledger'}` → `{t:'ledger', data: GodLedger}`: acts (`GodAct {id:'G#n', tick, power, target, text, witnesses}`), titles, god-image means, the benevolent/cruel/absent/just/capricious profile, ripples, prophecies, and `silenceUntil`.

**Rats**
- `RatsBlock.marks` (bits `MARK_BITS`: ear_notch, docked_tail, ear_tag, holy_mark, cursed_mark, scar).
- The delta's `house` field is the existing `RatsBlock.home` (−1 = none). Homes stay reserved/lite in v2 (the bible puts the full housing era in v2.5).
- `RatDetail`: `genome {loci (10 z-scores), coat genotype, bitterBlood, load, defects}`, `F`, `needs12`, `emotions12`, `godImage`, `marriage`, `marks[]`, `conditions[]`, `energy {stomach, fat, protein, vitality, thirst}`. `lastDecision.why` is always filled.

**Society and king**
- `SocietyStats`: `ga`, `misinformation`, `wrongfulConvictions`, `godImage` means, `arrangedShare`, `housedShare`, `casteIndex` (reserved), `atheistShare`, `ledger` (food-energy conservation, FU), `diseases` (SEIR counts per pathogen) and `conditions` (mental-illness counts). `trust` and `gini` already existed.
- `KingInfo.board {sockets[36], provenance[36], sealed[36]}`, `KingInfo.court {plate_bearer, herald, guard_captain, advisor, consort?, priest?}` (rat ids) and `KingInfo.stance` ('faithful' | 'repentant' | 'defiant'). `stanceToGod` keeps the finer six-way value.
- `Env`: `wagonInDays`, `marketDay`, `festiveSpill`, `binFood`, `ga`, and `rung` (the Giants' escalation: v2 reaches `traps` and `bait`). `HazardKind` adds `trap`.

**God powers (bible L12.3 v2 set)** — added: `punish_king` (params.punishment: pain | illness | terror | favourite, or omit to let fate pick), `sign` (params.text, at x,y), `voice` (params.text), `spring` (x,y), `mark` (rat; params.mark holy|cursed), `pardon` (rat), `judge` (rat: the truth of their last crime or accusation is shown), `reveal_law` (params.law: the king understands one button), `illuminate` (rat; params.tech), `silence` (params.days: a vow of silence; grace regenerates ×1.5 while it holds, broken by any act). `GodPowerInfo` adds `targetKind` and `halo` (an open prayer asks for this power). The older `punish` stays as the general form (any rat).

**Init and save**: `NewGameOpts.contentSettings` is echoed as `init.contentSettings`. `saved.header` is a `SaveHeader {magic:'RATG', saveVersion, contractVersion, seed, tick, created, contentSettings, godMode}`.
